# Backhouse

**The back office for real estate agents, teams, and brokerages.**

Backhouse is an AI-powered real estate platform that helps agents from lead to close. Transactions, contacts, documents, and compliance live in one place, connected to your email, calendar, and the MLS. Your assistant works in the background, tracking deadlines, drafting emails, prepping documents, and flagging what needs attention.

### What it does

- **AI assistant** – ask for help by text, email, or chat, with nothing to configure
- **Transaction management** – the contract lands and Backhouse pulls the dates and tracks every deadline
- **CRM** – emails logged, contacts updated, and every deal's stage in view
- **Client portal** – every step, date, and document in one place, under your brokerage's brand
- **Compliance review** – files checked against your brokerage's rules before they reach your broker
